CreaseMetrics is a site I built to analyze the stats of NHL goaltenders. Currently it has leaderboards of top goalies across the league as well as individual stat pages.
I built the frontend with `React` and the backend API with `Golang`.

I plan to add more advanced statistics and other features including:
- A heatmap of all the shots a goalie has faced, determining which areas of the ice they struggle with
- Goalie comparisons
- Comparing different team's goaltending tandems
