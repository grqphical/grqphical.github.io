## About Me
I am a software developer from Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada currently pursuing my Bachelors of Science in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University. Besides programming,
I have a 1st Dan Black Belt in Tae Kwon Do, in which I have been training in since 2017. In my free time you can find me either playing strategy
video games (most likely Civilization VI), prepping for my next Dungeons and Dragons campaigin, or taking pictures with my DSLR camera.

My main areas of focus in computer science are backend development and systems programming, however recently I have been dabbling with a little
bit of machine learning and artificial intelligence.

Currently I am a Software Developer for the [SFU Racerbot](https://sfuracerbot.ca) design team. We are building 1/10th autonomous scale race cars
using NVIDIA Jetsons, CUDA, and ROS2. My role is to help develop the firmware to ensure that all the components of the car can communicate
with each other efficiently and reliably. I also built the team's website.

### My Origin Story
Growing up I was surrounded by technology, whether it was my family's Nintendo Wii or the old laptop I was given to play around with. I'd always
experimented with computers but never really started coding until I was in middle school. There was this coding activity we did where we were
given these small circuitboards that had two buttons, an array of LEDs, and a microcontroller that were called Microbits. You could program them
using a visual programming language (basically Scratch). I instantly became addicted to building little games and programs on them; I felt like a god
being able to control this tiny little circuitboard. From there, I started learning Lua to make games on Roblox, learned Python to actually learn
to code, learned C++ for my high school robotics team, and now I am trying to code for a career.
